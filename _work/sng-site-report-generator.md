opportunity_lead: Monthly agency spend, for work a machine could do.
opportunity:
  - SNG need data-heavy reports for each site they consider for development. Until now, they sent the job out to an agency, who had someone pull the numbers and fill in the same template every time, paying on a per-report basis.
  - None of the information was hard to get, it sat in easily accessible systems that machines can directly interact with. The agency wasn't researching anything — they were gathering data, copy-pasting, formatting, and writing a few paragraphs around the numbers.
  - Same template, same sources, same shape every time. That's the kind of job worth automating.

what_we_did_lead: Two weeks, start to finish.
what_we_did:
  - We built something that collects the data itself. Every source the agency was pulling from, gathered in one go behind the scenes. All the team need to do is give the coordinates of the site, and the automation does the rest.
  - Then AI reads the data and writes the commentary. Not the numbers — those come straight from the source and never get touched. It writes the narrative around them, in the style SNG's reports already used.
  - It fills in their existing document template — builds maps, presents data in charts, adds context — and exports a PDF. Same report, same look, same place in their process & no additional work or responsibilities for the team.

result_lead: Minutes, and a few pounds of compute.
result:
  - SNG can now run a report whenever they need. It comes back in minutes and costs less than a coffee.
  - The bigger change is that reports stopped being rationed. When it costs a lot of money, you only order it when you're already fairly sure. When it costs almost nothing, you just run it — you find things you wouldn't have gone looking for.
