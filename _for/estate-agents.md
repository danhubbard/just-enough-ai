  - title: Copy that reads like you wrote it
    lead: Most AI writing still sounds like AI writing. Generic tools create generic copy. You can spot it a mile off.
    paragraphs:
      - With bespoke tools, you can have AI write like it knows your company. If you're the kind of person who says "tucked away" instead of "secluded," so does your copywriting tool.   - title: Enquiry triage — know who to call back first
    lead: Portals, email, WhatsApp, the website form. Buyers expect a quick reply. AI can read the pile so your negotiators talk to the right people.
    paragraphs:
      - Incoming messages get summarised — what they're asking, which property, how urgent it sounds, whether they've mentioned budget or timescales. Not a auto-reply that annoys people. A briefing for your team before they pick up the phone.
      - The goal isn't to remove the human conversation. Estate agency is a people business and it should stay that way. It's to stop your best negotiators spending twenty minutes reading threads before they work out who's serious.
      - We hook into how you already receive enquiries — we don't ask you to change portals or move to a new CRM. Small integration, immediate clarity.
  - title: Follow-ups and vendor updates, drafted in your tone
    lead: Viewing feedback, price reduction emails, "just checking in" messages — the writing that keeps deals moving but nobody enjoys doing.
    paragraphs:
      - After a viewing, your negotiator dictates two sentences in the car. The system turns that into a proper vendor update or buyer follow-up, in the tone your agency uses — formal, friendly, whatever fits.
      - Same for chasing solicitors, confirming appointments, or letting a vendor know about interest levels. Drafts land in your inbox or CRM ready to review and send. Minutes, not a blank email staring contest.
      - Again, nothing goes out without a human checking it. This is about removing the friction of starting from scratch, not automating relationships.
