The crux

You have a feeling that AI could probably help, but you're not sure where or how.

Maybe you've tried ChatGPT or even set up an automation with Zapier, but they didn't quite deliver what you needed.

Everyone's telling you to adopt AI. But nobody's telling you which tools or how to implement them. So it stays on the list of things you'll get to.

There's a job in your week that eats three hours and nobody enjoys. A machine could do most of it. There's also four ways to do it that don't make sense in your business and would waste your money.

Knowing which is which is the point of our AI Opportunity Review.

How it works

A couple of hours of your time, and we'll deliver a short, ranked report.

01

Ninety minutes on how you actually work

Not a demo. A conversation.

We go through a normal week with you. Where the hours go. What gets repeated. What gets dropped when you're busy. What you're still doing by hand that feels like it could be automated.

We'll want to see the real thing. The actual spreadsheet, the inbox, the process.

02

We list and rank the opportunities

Clarity, not more confusion.

We work out what's solvable now, with tools that exist and work today.

Everything we find gets a rough implementation cost and a rough saving. Some are an afternoon's work. Some are a proper project. Some turn out to be an off-the-shelf tool that does the job.

We look just as hard for what you shouldn't do. Talking you out of one bad project pays for this several times over.

Worth knowing: we rank by what pays you back - in money, time or sanity - not by what's interesting to build or the latest shiny thing. You get the same answer whether it's "build this" or "buy that off the shelf."

03

A short, ranked report. It's yours.

Problems, solutions and trade-offs for you to act on.

Everything we found, ranked by payoff against effort. What it'd cost. What it'd save. What to do first and what to leave. Plus what we ruled out, and why.

Most businesses we look at have a few small things worth doing. Usually one of them stands out as the best value for money and effort, and pays for itself within a couple of months.

We walk you through it on a call, or in person if you're near us, and answer whatever you ask. Then it's yours to keep and act on — build it in-house, hand it to someone else, or hire us. No retainer, no obligation, nothing recurring.
