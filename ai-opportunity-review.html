How it works

Two weeks start to finish. About two hours of your time.

01

Ninety minutes on how you work

Not a demo. Questions.

We go through a normal week with you. Where the hours go. What gets repeated. What gets dropped when you're busy. What you're still doing by hand because nobody got round to fixing it.

We'll want to see the real thing. The actual spreadsheet, the actual inbox, the actual process. Not a description of it.

02

We go away and think about it

This is the bit you're paying for.

We work out what's solvable now, with tools that exist and work today. Not what might be possible in eighteen months.

Everything we find gets a rough build cost and a rough saving. Some are an afternoon's work. Some are a proper project. Some turn out to be a £40-a-month tool that does the job — far better to know that than spend twenty grand finding out.

We look just as hard for what you shouldn't do. Talking you out of one bad project pays for this several times over.

Worth knowing: we rank by what pays you back, not by what's interesting to build. You get the same answer whether it's "build this" or "buy that off the shelf."

03

A short, ranked report. It's yours.

Short enough to read in one sitting. Every line is a decision.

Everything we found, ranked by payoff against effort. What it'd cost. What it'd save. What to do first and what to leave. Plus what we ruled out, and why.

Most businesses we look at have three or four things worth doing. Usually one of them pays for itself within a couple of months.

We walk you through it on a call and answer whatever you ask. Then it's yours to act on — build it in-house, hand it to someone else, or hire us. No retainer, nothing recurring.
